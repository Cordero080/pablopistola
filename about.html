About

Pablo Cordero

The Philosophy: Architecture & Intuition

I build systems that bridge the gap between complex engineering and intuitive navigation. I move beyond "bootstrap boilerplate" layouts because the medium is just as important as the product. To me, aesthetic is a functional requirement; it is the force that ensures user engagement and clarifies the system's intent.

The Evolution: From Artist to Architect

My background is a continuous practice of training perception and structure across different domains:

  • The Artist & Draftsman: I approach development the same way I approach a drawing—by laying out a sketch, following an intuitive hunch, and then organizing the chaos into a functional system.
  • The Linguist: I leverage universal symbols and icons to bypass language barriers and create an immediate, intuitive connection between the user and the software.
  • The Martial Artist: My training as a Goju-Ryu instructor taught me the value of discipline and traditional structure. I translated this directly into the Dojo App, a full-stack system that models the traditional kata, bunkai, and dan ranking requirements of martial arts practice.

The Practice: Creative Technology & Applied AI

My work focuses on combining motion graphics, interactive 3D, and deep system architecture into a unified experience.

Creative Foundation: Tamagotchi Transcendence

This project established my core methodology: building interactive systems where art and code are inseparable. It features a comprehensive Blender → FBX → Three.js pipeline with 32 custom animations. The simulation uses visual metaphors—light, color, and size—to track evolution, supported by a hidden "Battle Arena" that explores varied 3D environments from neopunk cityscapes to traditional Japanese dojos.

Featured Research: Pneuma

Pneuma is a cognitive orchestration layer—a personality architecture for LLMs. I built it to see what would happen if an AI was forced to inhabit its own contradictions. Using a collision engine of 46 philosophical archetypes, Pneuma doesn't just "chat"; it metabolizes thinking methods to hold paradox, detect loops, and provide genuine dialectical synthesis.

System Design: Numeneon

As the UI and System Architect for Numeneon, I designed the application scaffolding and core task architecture. I engineered a reverse-timeline "River" feed designed to mitigate digital congestion, creating a "Dojo-style" social platform that prioritizes daily learning and cultural discovery over traditional scroll-based noise.

Technical Experimentation: NUCAT

NUCAT is a real-time ASCII point cloud renderer. I designed and rigged the character, then engineered a system that explodes the model into ~20,000 GPU-efficient particles. It features a layered effects system—hover, wave, spiral, and entropy—that maintains skeleton animation while the "body" of the character transcends into a point cloud. This project demonstrates my ability to handle complex InstancedMesh rendering and real-time post-processing.

The Graduate's "Proof of Work"

Across 43 combined projects, I have maintained a high-velocity development cycle with 3,000+ contributions. Whether I am modeling traditional discipline (Dojo App), biological growth (Tamagotchi Transcendence), or cognitive friction (Pneuma), my work is a testament to the "plumbing" required for high-concept engineering.
